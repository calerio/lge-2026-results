      How this is calculated

      DA share is DA votes divided by all valid votes on the same ballot in the same place. The live results page adds the ward and PR ballots together, so its DA share differs slightly from the PR-ballot figures here. Change is in percentage points (pts): 20% to 25% is +5 pts. Vote change is the difference in DA votes. Totals for several places add the votes first and then divide; they never average percentages.

      A municipality is compared only if no voting district moved into or out of it between the two elections. A ward is compared only if it is made of exactly the same voting districts as one earlier ward; a ward with the same number but different districts is left out. Province and South Africa totals include every municipality, because those outer boundaries did not change.

      Blue means the DA's share went up, orange that it went down. Orange does not stand for any party. Arrows show the size of the change at each place; they do not show people moving.

      “Where the DA started from” groups comparable municipalities by the DA's share in the earlier election and adds up their votes. The IEC files do not say who governed each council, so these groups are not a measure of where the DA governed.

      Source: IEC detailed results files (results.elections.org.za). Not affiliated with the IEC.
