How it works

Areas that report early often vote differently from the ones still to come, so we compare each district with how it voted in 2021. The method follows the general idea behind the CSIR's election-night projections, in a simpler form of our own that hasn't been checked against theirs.

  1. Every voting district is grouped by its 2021 result: province, DA share and ANC share.
  2. For each counted district, we measure how much each party's share and the number of ballots changed since 2021.
  3. Each uncounted district takes the change from a counted district in its group, applied to its own 2021 result.
  4. We repeat that 2,000 times for each metro and 600 times nationally. The middle result is the estimate, and 90% of the runs fall inside the range shown.

An estimate only appears once at least 90% of the uncounted vote has five or more counted districts with a similar 2021 result. "Counted" means voting districts reported, which isn't the same as votes counted.

The figures are shares of all ballots combined, like the rest of the site. Parties and the media usually quote the party-list ballot alone, so their numbers can differ by about a point. We only estimate vote share. Council seats and control depend on how ward and list seats are allocated and on coalitions, which we don't model.

Tested on 2021

We replayed the 2021 count. At 10%, 25%, 50% and 75% of districts in, the model saw only those districts and the 2016 results, and estimated the final 2021 result. We ran it once in a typical reporting order and once in a worst-case order, with the most pro-DA areas first.

Source: IEC results feed (results.elections.org.za). The estimates are ours, not the IEC's. Not affiliated with the IEC.
