    How this is calculated

    All counts are IEC votes on the municipal party-list (PR) ballot. DA share is DA votes divided by all valid votes on that ballot. Totals add votes first and then divide.

    The split of a vote change: with D for DA votes, V for valid votes and s = D/V, the change D₁ − D₀ equals a share part (s₁ − s₀) × (V₀ + V₁)/2 plus a ballot-volume part (V₁ − V₀) × (s₀ + s₁)/2. The two parts add up exactly. This is arithmetic on the counts. It cannot tell who switched party or who stayed home.

    A municipality is compared only if no voting district moved across its boundary between the two elections; a ward only if it has exactly the same voting districts as an earlier ward. Province and national totals include every municipality. Party names are matched exactly, with one listed rename: “Vryheidsfront Plus” (2016, 2021) is the same party as “Vryheidsfront Plus | Freedom Front Plus” (2026).
