    How this is calculated

    All counts are IEC votes on the municipal party-list (PR) ballot. DA share is DA votes divided by all valid votes on that ballot. Totals add votes first and then divide. Changes in share are in percentage points (pts): 20% to 25% is +5 pts. The live results page adds the ward and PR ballots together, so its DA share differs slightly from this page.

    The split of a vote change: with D for DA votes, V for valid votes and s = D/V, the change D₁ − D₀ equals a share part (s₁ − s₀) × (V₀ + V₁)/2 plus a valid-votes part (V₁ − V₀) × (s₀ + s₁)/2. The two parts add up exactly; the valid-votes part is rounded to whole votes and the share part is the rest. “More people voting” means more valid votes on this ballot, which moves with both turnout and the number of registered voters. This is arithmetic on the counts. It cannot tell who switched party or who stayed home.

    A municipality is compared only if no voting district moved across its boundary between the two elections; a ward only if it has exactly the same voting districts as an earlier ward. Province and national totals include every municipality. Party names are matched exactly, with one listed rename: “Vryheidsfront Plus” (2016, 2021) is the same party as “Vryheidsfront Plus | Freedom Front Plus” (2026).

    Source: IEC detailed results files (results.elections.org.za). Not affiliated with the IEC.
